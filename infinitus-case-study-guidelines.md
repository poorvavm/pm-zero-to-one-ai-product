# Infinitus Case Study Guidelines

## Context

Infinitus is exploring a new product offering for pharmaceutical manufacturers. The opportunity centers on evaluating completed conversations—across voice, SMS, email, and other channels—as human staff or Infinitus AI agents engage with patients and prescribing physicians on behalf of our customers.

The core problems this product would address include:

- Detecting potential adverse reactions reported by patients or providers
- Identifying deviations from standard operating procedures by staff or AI agents
- Surfacing insights and analytics to help customers manage compliance across patient support operations

## Leadership Priorities

Leadership has signaled that speed and execution are the priority. There is strong appetite to have a pilot-ready product in **3 months**. This is not a soft target.

You will need to make hard calls about what gets built, deferred, or validated before a single line of code is written. How you think about sequencing and scope under this pressure is as important as what you ultimately decide to build.

## Lead the Discussion

Come prepared to lead a working session with engineers, UX designers, and cross-functional stakeholders. We're looking for your thinking — not a polished presentation.

There are no required sections. Structure the conversation however you think is most useful. At a minimum, we'd expect the discussion to cover:

- Why this problem is worth solving now, and for whom
- What you'd actually build — and what you'd deliberately leave out
- What would the product experience be? How would you approach defining this?
- What would customer onboarding look like?
- How you'd run a pilot given the constraints above
- How you'd know if the MVP was working

The team will ask questions throughout.

## Anchor Question

> *"A competitor already offers conversation analytics for call centers. Why would a pharmaceutical manufacturer buy this from Infinitus instead — and what would make you confident enough to prioritize building it?"*
